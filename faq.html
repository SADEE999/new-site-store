PLAYER SUPPORT / KNOWLEDGE BASE

FREQUENT QUESTIONS.

How do I place an order?

Choose a product and plan, enter your contact details and payment reference, then submit the order.

When will I receive my key?

After the store admin confirms the actual payment and issues your license, the key appears in Order Status.

How do I check an order?

Open Order Status and enter the order code and checkout email.

What if my order stays pending?

Keep your payment receipt and contact support with your order code. Never submit a false payment reference.
